Requirements

  • CPU: 64-bit x86 (x86_64).
  • Firmware: UEFI. Secure Boot supported through shim.
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM minimum.
  • Storage: the minimum space shown by the Debian installer.
  • Graphics: integrated GPU —Intel HD 2500 validated— or an NVIDIA GPU supported by the Debian drivers.
  • Display and network: a TV or monitor, and the PC on the same local network as your phone (Wi-Fi or wired).
  • Base: Debian 13 stable amd64 minimal, with the Xorg/Openbox session and Firefox ESR.

Other setups may work but are not guaranteed.

Download and install

  1. Download the ISO from GitHub Releases together with SHA256SUMS, and verify the image.
  2. Write the ISO to a USB drive with Rufus, Ventoy or dd.
  3. Boot the PC from the USB drive in UEFI mode and choose the Install entry from the boot menu.
  4. When it finishes, the TV shows the LinuxRemotePlayer panel with a PIN and a QR code.
Reboot required for control: the system adds your user to the input group for the virtual keyboard and mouse. That permission applies after a reboot (or after closing and reopening the session).

Pairing your phone

The TV panel shows a 6-digit PIN and a QR code:

  • Scan the QR with your phone camera, or
  • open the panel address in the phone browser and type the PIN.

The phone is paired with a local token. You can repeat the process for every phone in the house; they can all control the TV at the same time.

Control from your phone

  • iPhone / iPad: open the panel page in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, without browser chrome. On iOS you use the PWA; there is no APK.
  • Android: download the 1.0.0 APK, which is the Android remote-control app. You can also install the PWA from Chrome (⋮ menu → Install app).
  • Control: touchpad, keyboard, navigation, apps and search. Everything runs on your local network, never through the internet.
Computer certificate: the APK accepts the computer certificate without validating it. Install it only from this page and use it only on your local network.

Privacy and licenses

  • 100% local. Control and voice run on your network: no accounts, no cloud services, no quotas and no telemetry. The PC works without internet.
  • Protected access. Nobody controls the TV without the PIN or pairing link; every connection is authenticated with a token.
  • Free software. LRP OS is free software under GNU GPL v3. See LICENSE, NOTICE and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.

Uninstall

If you installed LRP OS as the whole system, reinstall the operating system you want to use. The app configuration lives on the PC and is never sent to a service.

Troubleshooting

The phone can't find the PC

  • Make sure both are on the same network (guest networks usually isolate devices).
  • If your-pc.local does not respond, try the direct IP shown by the panel (https://192.168.x.x:8000).

The browser shows a security warning

This is expected: the panel uses a certificate generated by the PC. Accept the exception once, or download the CA certificate from the panel itself.

Touchpad or keyboard not responding right after install

A reboot is missing: hardware permissions apply after restarting the PC (or closing and reopening the session). It only happens once.
