Changelog

What each version brings. LinuxRemotePlayer release history.

1.0.0 starts the open source era (GNU GPL v3). Entries above the 1.0.0 line describe the archived commercial product in the legacy/2.0 branch.

v1.9.1

July 30, 2026
  • Fixed on iPhone: reopening the app no longer pushes the screen down — no empty band at the top, and the bottom buttons stay reachable.
  • Fixed: the "Update" button no longer errors out when a log file created by another user was left on the machine.

v1.9.0

July 30, 2026
  • All four themes are now free for everyone. Day, Night, Café and Anime ship with the install; they are no longer part of Pro.
  • Voice turns itself on when you register your licence, and you can turn it off any time from Settings.
  • Updates no longer leave the service down. The updater used to interrupt itself and the app stayed unresponsive until restarted by hand.
  • Reinstalling no longer breaks the pairing with your phones.
  • Uninstalling leaves the machine clean: certificates, keys and credentials are removed instead of being left on disk.
  • Installation no longer stops halfway and tells you which step is missing.

v1.8.9

July 24, 2026
  • AI voice through our own server: the model keys no longer live on your PC. Commands are processed server-side, with a daily quota per licence.
  • 1-device licence with self-service transfer: activate your key on another PC and the app asks, then moves it over for you.
  • New visual identity across the app, the installer and the website.

v1.8.0

July 20, 2026
  • Big internal cleanup ahead of launch: lighter install package and a simpler, more reliable voice engine.
  • Documentation and website corrected and brought up to date.

v1.7.8

July 20, 2026
  • Fixed: the "Voice commands" guide in Settings didn't appear in some cases; it now always shows.

v1.7.5 – v1.7.7

July 18-19, 2026
  • More robust voice: fixed the microphone on iPhone, voice errors now visible on screen, and a command guide available in Settings.
  • More system apps: the detector now also finds apps like the file manager or the terminal, so you can launch them from your phone.
  • The phone app now picks up updates automatically on every release.

v1.7.0 – v1.7.4

July 14-18, 2026
  • Firefox as the TV browser: more compatible, with ad blocking (uBlock Origin) included automatically.
  • Real-time latency indicator in the status bar.
  • More robust installation (no certificate hangs) and a fail-safe auto-updater.
  • New installer option to keep the TV screen from turning off on its own.
  • Design fixes for iPhone (16:9 screens, icons, notch).

v1.6.0

July 15, 2026
  • Spanish keyboards: support for ES and LATAM layouts (ñ, accents and AltGr symbols).
  • The Home button now truly leaves a clean desktop, and "back" navigates without rewinding the video.
  • Fewer latency spikes in the remote.

v1.5.0 and earlier

July 2026
  • "Cozy Media", the app's design language, is born.
  • 6-digit PIN pairing, TV status panel, welcome tour, built-in feedback form and the theme system.
  • Dedicated support for KDE Plasma Bigscreen.
