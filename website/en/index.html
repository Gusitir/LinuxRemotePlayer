LRP OS 1.0.1 · GNU GPL v3

Turn your PC into a Smart TV controlled from your phone.

LRP OS is a Debian ISO that boots straight into a full-screen panel. Your phone is the remote: touchpad, keyboard, apps and search. No cloud, no accounts, no license and no telemetry.

Download ISO 1.0.1 Android APK

Every release publishes the ISO with SHA256SUMS to verify the download. Android uses the APK; iOS uses the PWA.

What it is

Your PC as a TV, your phone as the remote.

A complete x86_64 distribution with no external service dependencies.

No cloud

Control and voice run on your local network. No accounts, no third-party services, no telemetry: the PC works without internet.

Control from your phone

Touchpad, keyboard, navigation and apps in one place. Android installs the APK; iOS uses the PWA.

Free software

LRP OS is free software under GNU GPL v3. The source and third-party licenses are published in the public repository.

The remote

The remote on your phone

Your phone is the remote control. Pair it with a 6-digit PIN or a QR code; it only works inside your local network.

Android · APK

iPhone / iPad · PWA

Requirements

Compatible hardware.

Declared support for the base system and the reference machine.

CPU

64-bit x86 (x86_64).

Firmware

UEFI. Secure Boot supported through shim.

Memory

4 GB RAM minimum.

Storage

The minimum space shown by the Debian installer.

Graphics

Integrated GPU —Intel HD 2500 validated— or an NVIDIA GPU supported by the Debian drivers.

Display and network

A TV or monitor, and a local network (Wi-Fi or wired) next to your phone.

Base: Debian 13 stable amd64 minimal · Session: Xorg/Openbox · Browser: Firefox ESR

Other setups may work but are not guaranteed.

Install

Three steps.

On compatible hardware, without installing Linux by hand.

1

Write the ISO

Use Rufus, Ventoy or dd to write the image to a USB drive. Verify the download with SHA256SUMS.

2

Boot and choose “Install”

Boot the PC from the USB drive in UEFI mode and choose the installation entry from the boot menu.

3

Open the panel and scan the QR

When it finishes, the TV shows the panel with a PIN. Scan the QR code with your phone to pair and start controlling.
