LRP OS 1.0.0 · GNU GPL v3

Turn your PC into a Smart TV controlled from your phone.

LRP OS is a Debian ISO that boots straight into a media center. Your phone is the remote: touchpad, keyboard, apps and search. No cloud, no accounts, no license and no telemetry.

What it is

Your PC as a TV, your phone as the remote.

A complete x86_64 distribution with no external service dependencies.

●

No cloud

Control and voice run on your local network. No accounts, no third-party services, no telemetry: the PC does not need internet to work.

●

Full control from your phone

Touchpad, keyboard, navigation and apps in one place. Install it as a PWA on iPhone/iPad or as an APK on Android, then pair with a 6-digit PIN.

●

Free software

LRP OS is free software under GNU GPL v3. The source and third-party licenses are published in the public repository.

Requirements

Compatible hardware.

Declared support for the base system and the reference machine.

Platform

x86_64 with UEFI. Secure Boot supported through shim.

Memory

8 GB RAM recommended.

Graphics

Integrated GPU, or NVIDIA RTX 3060 12 GB as the certified GPU.

Base: Debian 13 stable amd64 minimal · Session: Xorg/Openbox · Browser: Firefox ESR

You also need a TV or monitor and your local network (Wi-Fi or wired). Other setups may work but are not guaranteed.

Install

Three steps.

On compatible hardware, without installing Linux by hand.

1

Write the ISO

Use Rufus, Ventoy or dd to write the image to a USB drive. Verify the download with SHA256SUMS.

2

Boot and pick “Install”

Boot the PC from the USB drive in UEFI mode and choose the installation entry from the boot menu.

3

Open the panel and scan the QR

When it finishes, the TV shows the panel with a PIN. Scan the QR code with your phone to pair and start controlling.

Control

The phone you already own.

No remote to charge and no mandatory app store.

  • iPhone / iPad: open the panel page in Safari and choose “Share → Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android: install the PWA from Chrome (⋮ menu → Install app). The APK ships in the next release.
  • Pairing: 6-digit PIN or QR code from the TV panel.
  • No cloud: all control traffic is encrypted inside your local network.
