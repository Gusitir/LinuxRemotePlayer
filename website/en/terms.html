Requirements

A 64-bit x86 PC (x86_64) with UEFI (Secure Boot supported through shim), 4 GB RAM minimum, an integrated GPU —Intel HD 2500 validated— or an NVIDIA GPU supported by the Debian drivers, a TV or monitor, and the PC on the same local network as your phone. Storage needs the minimum space shown by the Debian installer. The base system is Debian 13 stable amd64 minimal with the Xorg/Openbox session and Firefox ESR. Other setups may work but are not guaranteed.

Privacy

Control and voice run on your local network. There is no telemetry and no cloud service, and the PC does not need internet to work. See the privacy section of the documentation.
